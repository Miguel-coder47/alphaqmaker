How it works

Your files are read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to an alphaQmaker server.

1

Add your files

Choose 1 to 5 PDF or PowerPoint files, and pick the slides you want, like “12 to 30”.

2

Get your questions

Use a free chat AI by copy and paste, or the built-in no-AI mode that turns real sentences into fill-in-the-blank questions.

3

Practice and review

Take the quiz, see your score against a passing grade, and flip through flashcards of what you missed.

What makes it different

Seven question types

Multiple choice, true or false, choose all that apply, fill in the blank, matching, enumeration, and modified true or false.

Careful answer checking

Capital letters, spacing and number formats never count against you, and a one-letter typo is forgiven. A close but unclear answer asks you to decide instead of silently marking it wrong.

Fair, exact scoring

Partial credit where it makes sense, and a passing grade compared with exact fractions, so 59.99% never rounds up to a pass.

Weak-spot flashcards

Questions you miss come back as flashcards until you get them right first time.

Built to be trusted

Every generated question must point to a sentence that really appears on the cited page, and bad questions can be reported and hidden.

Private and free

No account, no tracking. Your quizzes and history stay in your browser, with one-click backup and restore.

About this project

alphaQmaker is a student project built to practice for quizzes, seatworks, and final exams. The engine behind it (answer checking, scoring, file reading, validation) is plain JavaScript with an automated test suite, and the whole project is open source.

  • Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no framework
  • PDF and PowerPoint reading in the browser with pdf.js and JSZip
  • Automated tests for the answer checker, scoring rules, and file pipeline
